# Fraud awareness and prevention at the branch

## Purpose

Branch staff are the first line of defense against social engineering, identity theft, and unauthorized fund transfers. This policy outlines common fraud patterns in Pakistan banking, mandatory verification rules, and immediate escalation protocols.

## Pattern 1: Social engineering and impersonation

Fraudsters frequently call or visit branches claiming to be regulatory officials, head office auditors, or IT support personnel asking for passwords, system overrides, or customer PINs.

Rule: Head office and IT staff will never ask a branch officer for customer credentials, one-time passwords (OTPs), or system passwords over the phone. Any such request must be treated as an attempt to compromise the bank and reported immediately to the Information Security Incident Desk.

## Pattern 2: Digital banking and OTP diversion

A common scheme involves a fraudster obtaining a customer's CNIC copy and attempting to change the registered mobile number at the counter. Once the number is updated, the fraudster registers for mobile banking and intercepts OTPs to empty accounts.

Rule: Mobile number updates require:

1. Physical presence of the account holder with their original CNIC.
2. Biometric thumb impression verification matched via NADRA.
3. Confirmation of the previous three transactions on the account.
   A mobile number change can never be processed on behalf of a third party, family member, or through an authority letter without written sign-off from the Branch Manager.

## Pattern 3: Cheque cloning and altered instruments

Altered cheques typically target amounts and beneficiary names. Signs of alteration include:

- Inconsistent ink shade or pen stroke thickness.
- Chemical washing marks around the payee line or amount box.
- Differences between the written words and numerical figures.

Rule: For any clearing or counter cheque exceeding five hundred thousand rupees (PKR 500,000), telephone confirmation with the drawer using the registered contact number is mandatory before passing the instrument. Never use contact numbers handwritten on the back of the cheque.

## Escalation and reporting

When fraud is suspected:

1. Do not alert the suspicious individual at the counter.
2. Inform the Branch Operations Manager using the designated quiet signal.
3. Freeze the affected account immediately in the core banking system to prevent outgoing transfers.
4. Log an incident report with the Compliance and Anti-Money Laundering unit within two hours.
